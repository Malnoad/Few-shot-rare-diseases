Few-shot OCT × CycleGAN

Figures, an attribution-controlled protocol and a count-matched arm design
for the Yoo et al. 2021 reproduction

Research proposal • Working figures • Version 1.0 • 6 November 2026

Abstract. The reproduction of Yoo et al. (2021) trains a frozen Inception-v3 with a softmax head on nine OCT classes, five of them rare. At seed 42 the CycleGAN variant reaches 92.95% against the paper's 92.1%, but over five seeds it is not separated from basic augmentation (−0.35 ± 2.77 pp). Three findings limit what any later GAN gain can mean: the GAN quality filter selects images in the classifier's own feature space; rare and common images stay separable in that space after format and framing normalisation (AUC 0.991); and the GAN arm adds rare images as well as GAN content. This pack draws the pipeline as built, the results of record, the evidence behind those three limits, the locked C1 decision protocol, and a proposed ladder of arms in which each step changes one thing. The only measured numbers are the seed-42 run, seeds 1–5, the C0 gate and the C5 counts; no C1, C2 or C3 outcome exists.

1  Pipeline as reproduced

Figure 1 separates images (teal) from the classifier (blue). Images reach the head only as frozen features, so the head is the only trained part. Test labels connect only to evaluation.

2  Results of record

The seed-42 ordering (gan > none > basic) does not survive five seeds. basic at seed 42 is its lowest seed. Both augmentations get more rare test images right than none (Fig. 2c), but none already gets far more than the paper's 3/22.

3  Why rare-class numbers need care

C0 asked whether normalising file format and framing removes the separation between rare and common images. It does not (Fig. 3a): the gate required AUC < 0.90 before training heads, and framing still gives 0.991. Framing removes the header-size cue (Fig. 3b) but not the separation, so disease and source stay confounded.

The automatic QC kept far fewer MacTel and RP images than the paper's 3,000 (Fig. 4a). C5 filled the v2 pool to 3,000 for C2/C3, at the cost of 12,508 new RP candidates for 2,168 kept (Fig. 4b). C1 keeps v1.

4  Proposed protocol: the C1 decision

C1 was locked on 27 October 2026, before any outcome. Figure 5 draws its stopping rule and the five-zone reading of the final interval. The precision strip shows why 20 pairs are not enough: at the five-seed SD, their 95% interval is still more than 1 pp wide on each side.

Primary hypothesis (C1). Over paired classifier seeds (fixed 250 epochs, final head), the accepted v1 GAN pool raises mean test accuracy over basic augmentation by at least 1 pp. Failure means the paper's GAN-over-basic claim is not reproduced at 1 pp; it does not show equivalence.

5  Proposed architecture: a single-change ladder of arms

C1 can show a GAN gain but not why. Its gan arm differs from basic in two ways at once: GAN content and up to 2,700 more rare fitting images per class. The ladder in Figure 6 changes one thing per step. Two arms are new; the rest already exist in C2. The rare-conditional readout costs no training: it restricts each head's prediction to the five rare classes on the 22 rare tests, so a rare-vs-common source cue cannot help. Source cues between the five rare classes can still help.

Proposed secondary hypothesis (A3 − A2). At an equal 5,000 rare images per class, 2,000 basic + 3,000 accepted GAN images beat 5,000 basic images on mean test accuracy over seeds 1–20. Failure narrows any GAN gain to image count.

A1 → A3 is C2 as designed: it changes GAN content and image count together. A2 − A1 isolates count; A3 − A2 isolates GAN content at equal count, which is the paper's claim. A3 − A4 asks whether the gain depends on the QC choosing images near real disease in the head's own feature space.

6  Decision rules and open feasibility

After C1. If L ≥ 1 pp, run A2 before claiming GAN content. If U < 1 pp, report that the GAN-over-basic claim is not reproduced at 1 pp, and treat C2 as a dose description. After C2 + A2. If accuracy rises with N but A2 matches A3, the gain is from image count. After A4. If A3 beats A4, the gain depends on feature-space selection, and the QC result must not be read as image quality. Always. Rare-class results stay source-confounded, and the test set shares patients with training; no outcome supports a clinical claim.

Feasibility (unverified). stage1_basic_aug.py has --n but no output-folder flag; A2 needs one so the committed 2,000 images stay fixed, and whether a 5,000 draw keeps the current 2,000 as a subset is unchecked. A4 needs the rejected v1/v2 candidates, and their presence on Drive is unchecked. A2 and A4 would add 40 heads to C2's 100; at the unmeasured C2 forecast of about 13.5 min per head, about 9 h.
